# In progress

These skills aren't ready yet, so they live outside `skills/` on purpose: the
installers only look in `skills/`, which means nothing in here gets installed
by `npx skills add bradfrost/skills`. Feel free to read along, but please
don't rely on anything here until it moves over to `skills/`.

- **`what-do-i-make-of-this`**: a thinking mirror for articles, hot takes, and
  hard questions. It's being reworked into a broader "help me make sense of
  this" skill.
